# Contributing to JiuJitsu Island RPG

First of all, thank you for taking the time to visit the project!

JiuJitsu Island RPG is currently a passion project developed and maintained by **Callum Carville**.

Although contributions are not currently being accepted, feedback from the community is always appreciated.

---

## 🐞 Reporting Bugs

If you discover a bug, please open a GitHub Issue and include:

- A description of the problem.
- Steps to reproduce it.
- Screenshots or videos if applicable.
- Your operating system and Python version.

---

## 💡 Feature Suggestions

Ideas and suggestions are always welcome.

If there's something you'd love to see in JiuJitsu Island RPG, feel free to open a Feature Request describing:

- The idea.
- Why it would improve the game.
- Any mock-ups or examples.

---

## ❤️ Feedback

Whether you've found a typo, spotted a balancing issue or simply enjoyed playing, I'd love to hear from you.

Community feedback plays a huge role in shaping future updates.

---

## 🔒 Code Contributions

At this stage, direct code contributions and pull requests are **not being accepted**.

The project is currently maintained by **Callum Carville** while development continues.

This may change in the future.

---

Thank you for supporting JiuJitsu Island RPG!
